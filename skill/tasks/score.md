# Task: Score

Judge a finished thumbnail. Used before publishing, or as a diagnosis when CTR is soft.

**Load:** `frameworks/four-cs.md`, `templates/thumbnail-review.md`. Read `context/channel-profile.md` and the corpus README if one exists.

---

## 1. Get the thing

Ask for the image file or URL, and the title it ships with. **Both.** Scoring a thumbnail without its title is scoring half the package, and most failures live in the seam between them.

If the user also has CTR and average view duration, ask for those. A thumbnail with high CTR and low retention has a different problem than one nobody clicks, and the fix is the opposite in each case.

---

## 2. Look at it twice

**Small first.** View it at roughly thumbnail scale before anything else. This is where the impression actually happens. Most failures are visible here and invisible at full size.

**Then full size.** Craft problems, edge quality, lighting, and spelling live here.

Do not skip the small view. Judging a thumbnail at full width is the single most common reason a bad one ships.

---

## 3. Score the four

Score each C out of 10 with a one-line reason. Then **take the lowest, not the average.** A frame at 10 composition and 4 curiosity is a 4, because nobody clicks it.

| C | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Subject on a third? Bottom right clear? One focal point? Does it survive at 120 pixels? |
| Color | Do subject and ground separate? Does the color match the promise? Flat and bright, or moody and muddy? |
| Clean Assets | Real, sharp, cut cleanly? Or a timeline still, a soft cutout, a generated approximation? |
| Curiosity | What question does this open? Which lever? Does the title repeat the thumbnail text? |

---

## 4. Diagnose against the niche

If a corpus exists, place the thumbnail in it:

- Is it running the current niche cliche? Being interchangeable with competitors is a failure even when the craft is good
- Which `concept_carrier` is it using, and how does that carrier rank in this niche?
- Note the corpus date. Over 90 days, say so and soften the claim

---

## 5. Verdict

Pass bar is 8.

**At or above 8:** say ship it, and name the one thing you would still change if there were time.

**Below 8:** name the failing C, give **one** specific fix, and show what the fixed version would look like as an ascii sketch. One fix, not a list. A list of five changes gets none of them done.

If CTR data came with it and CTR is fine but retention is poor, say the honest thing: the thumbnail is working and the video is not delivering on it. That is a content problem wearing a packaging costume, and no redesign fixes it.
